# Content & Messaging

This document defines all content used across the portfolio.

Content must be clear, direct, and outcome-focused.
No fluff. No generic statements.

---

# 1. Tone & Voice

* Clear
* Direct
* Professional
* Confident (not loud)

---

## Rules

* No buzzwords (“innovative”, “cutting-edge”)
* No long paragraphs
* No vague claims
* Every line must communicate value

---

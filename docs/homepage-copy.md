## VOICE RULES FOR ANY COPY YOU ADD LATER

**Do**
- First person. "I build," not "we deliver."
- Concrete nouns: brands, websites, mobile apps.
- Short sentences. One idea each.
- Say the trade-off out loud. "I don't take rush jobs" builds more trust than a list of virtues.

**Don't**
- "Solutions," "leverage," "seamless," "empower," "cutting-edge"
- "Structured digital systems" — too abstract, currently all over the site
- Claims you can't demonstrate on the same page
- Three adjectives where one noun would do

**The test:** read it aloud. If you wouldn't say it to a client sitting across from you, cut it.

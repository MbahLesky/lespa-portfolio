### Context (~100 words)
Qiroke is a tech collective I co-founded, offering software development, web development, branding, and digital marketing. The brand needed to represent a team, not a product — a mark flexible enough to hold across five services without picking a lane. I built the full identity system: a flowing "Q" with meaning constructed into the letterform itself, a four-variant color system for every background it would meet, and a homepage structured around the visitor's problem rather than a service list. Real stakeholder feedback — on weight, contrast, hierarchy, spacing — got addressed directly. The typography and color system are documented down to letter-spacing, so nothing downstream is guesswork.
